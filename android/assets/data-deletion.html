User Account & Data Deletion Instructions

Veylora respects your privacy rights. Follow the instructions below to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data from our systems.

Option 1: Direct In-App Account Deletion

  1. Open Veylora
    Launch the Veylora app on your Android device and log into your account.
  2. Navigate to Settings
    Tap the Settings icon on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Select Account & Privacy
    Scroll down to the Account & Privacy section and tap Delete Account & Personal Data.
  4. Confirm Account Deletion
    Read the deletion warning and tap Confirm Permanent Deletion. Your account, watchlists, ratings, and profile data will be removed instantly.

Option 2: Deleting Data via Facebook (Meta) Settings

If you created or logged into your Veylora account using Facebook Login, you can revoke application permissions and trigger data deletion via Facebook:

  1. Open Facebook Settings
    Go to your Facebook profile settings and select Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Apps and Websites
    Navigate to Apps and Websites to view all active integrations.
  3. Remove Veylora
    Find Veylora in the list, click Remove, and check the box requesting Facebook to notify us to delete your activity and data.

Option 3: Request Deletion via Email Support

If you no longer have access to the app or prefer assistance from our team, you can submit a manual deletion request:

Email Address: support@veylora.app

Subject Line: Data Deletion Request - Veylora

Please include your registered email address or account ID. Our support team will process your request and purge your data within 48 hours.

Email Support Team

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon confirmation of your deletion request, the following records are permanently erased from active storage:

  • Your name, email address, and authentication tokens
  • Your profile picture reference
  • Your entire movie and TV show watchlist
  • Your ratings, reviews, and custom collections
  • Your app settings and preference history
